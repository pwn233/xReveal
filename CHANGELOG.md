# Changelog

Each version's section is what xReveal shows in **What's new** after updating to it, and the body of its GitHub release. Keep it short, one line per change, in plain words.

## 0.1.0 – unreleased

- Play your console through a capture card with very little delay, on macOS and Windows.
- Fit, Stretch, Crop or Smart stretch the picture to any shape, with colour and picture controls.
- The card's own sound, with volume, mute and an A/V sync offset.
- Camera and Microphone are asked for separately, and you can play without sound.
- Settings slide in from the side (Tab); your choices are remembered for next time.
